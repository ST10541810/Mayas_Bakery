Our story

Who we are

A little bakery with a big heart.

Maya's Bakery began as a small home kitchen and grew into a local shop where fresh baking, friendly service and community come first.

Our mission is to bake fresh, affordable goods that make everyday moments special. We want customers to feel welcome whether they are picking up a loaf of bread or planning a celebration.

Our mission

To offer fresh, enjoyable and affordable baked goods with thoughtful customer service.

Our vision

To become a neighbourhood bakery that customers remember for quality, warmth and consistency.

Who we serve

Local residents, students, families and people ordering for birthdays and special events.
